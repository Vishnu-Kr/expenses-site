Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026. This policy covers the Expenses app for Android.

In short: everything you put in Expenses stays on your phone. The app has no account and no server, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

Your entries, accounts, categories, budgets, settings and receipt photos are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, including us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it.

SMS

Only when you turn on Read bank SMS, the app reads SMS on your phone to find messages from banks, cards and payment services (for example "₹450 debited from A/c XX1234 to Swiggy"). It turns those into entries. Personal SMS are skipped. SMS are read and processed only on your phone. They are never uploaded, sent or shared. You can turn this off at any time in More, or remove the SMS permission in Android's settings.

Notifications

Only when you turn on Read payment notifications and allow notification access, the app reads notifications from the payment, bank and messages apps you choose, to find payments. They are processed only on your phone and never leave it. Notifications from other apps are ignored.

Bank statements, photos and files

Statements you import, receipt photos you take or pick, and backups you restore are opened only on your phone. Passwords for locked statements are used once to open the file and are not saved.

Backups and exports

When you make a backup or an export, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only where you send it (for example your Google Drive or WhatsApp), under that service's own privacy policy.

Internet

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • download a small settings file from this website (it can tell old versions to update, or show a notice). Nothing about you or your data is sent. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) sees the request's IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement;
  • check for app updates and handle Premium purchases through Google Play. Purchases are processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details.

Crash reports

If you allow it in Android's settings, Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us (Android vitals). They contain technical details about the problem, not your entries or SMS.

Permissions

  • SMS: to read bank SMS (only when you turn it on).
  • Notification access: to read payment notifications (only when you turn it on).
  • Camera and photos: to attach receipt photos (only when you add one).
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder and "new bank SMS" alerts (only when you turn them on).
  • Fingerprint: to unlock the app (only when you turn on the app lock).

Children

Expenses is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Open an issue, or use the e-mail address on the app's Google Play page.
